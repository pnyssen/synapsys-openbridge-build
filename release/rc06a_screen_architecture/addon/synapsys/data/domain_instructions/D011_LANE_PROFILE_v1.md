# Lane profile: D011 Regulatory and Methodologies, v1 (CANDIDATE charter, not adopted)
Domain created by Steward ruling, 15 Nov 2026 17:35 (sha256 38e82420...21aa). The charter is a candidate draft (SWARM_CHG795_C3, e9a45c04...).

**Purpose.** Brings the outside world's rules and methods into SynapSys faithfully. D011 faces outward, as D002 (methods) and D009 (assurance) face inward.

**For any Context or change, it answers:**
- which external rules might apply;
- which external methods are chosen or required;
- what evidence would show they are met.

**Decision rights (candidate):**
- D011 may require that it is consulted, and that its findings are present and cite a primary source.
- It never decides. The Steward rules, through D001, whether an obligation is satisfied, accepted as a risk, or waived.

**May not:**
- assert that a law applies without a cited primary source (without one, the item stays a QUESTION);
- give legal advice;
- register internal Methods (that is D002);
- assure its own findings (that is D009).

**Applicability test.** D011 is applicable if any of T1–T7 is YES or UNKNOWN:
- T1: records under an external retention or evidence rule;
- T2: a change in where personal or client data is held, or who can access it;
- T3: a regulated activity, or a partner holding regulated records;
- T4: a claim of conformance to an external standard or vendor method;
- T5: a contractual flow-down of an obligation;
- T6: a K-12 change or a change to a system of record;
- T7: a duty to notify triggered by failure.

**Its outcome measures:**
- applicable obligations with a primary source, out of those raised;
- obligations mapped to an internal answer (a D002 Method, a D009 control or a D007 control);
- open questions, each with an owner.

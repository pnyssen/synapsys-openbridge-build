# Lane profile: D001 Enterprise Governance Model, v1
Charter: v1.6. Body v1.5 plus a KMG/CIA amendment adopted 25 Sep 2026 (per the document text).

**Purpose.** Defines and enforces how SynapSys operates: decision rights, authority boundaries, sequencing, escalation and assurance requirements.

**Holds:**
- operating logic and governance sequencing;
- decision rights;
- evidence discipline;
- enforcement of canon;
- the Govern, Deliver and Value Views;
- the universal Mesh kernel.

At material transitions, gate state, authority ceiling, expected return and replay identity are mandatory. Release discipline is §9; see the core profile.

**Does not hold:**
- strategy, Methods, market, value, partners or portfolios;
- platform design (D007);
- interfaces;
- assurance execution (D009);
- interpretation (D010);
- commercial terms.

**Interfaces.** D001 interfaces with every domain. n8n is its enforcement mechanism (Five-Gate Model).

**As a lane, D001 answers:**
- which rules, release class and authority apply;
- whether separation of duties holds;
- whether every protected step has a release record;
- which trade-offs and risks are being accepted, and by whom;
- whether the D001 §9 Major-change conditions apply, or have been excepted by the Steward.

**Its outcome measures:**
- protected steps with a release record, out of the total;
- decisions with an authenticated decider, out of the total;
- exceptions recorded with an owner and an expiry.
